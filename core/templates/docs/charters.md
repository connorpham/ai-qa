# Test charters

A charter is a mission with a timebox, not a script. It says what you are trying
to find out and where to look; what you actually do is decided while you are
looking. Scripted cases confirm what someone already thought of — charters find
what nobody did.

Charters come out of `/onboard` (from the risk map) and get refreshed by
`/regress`. Each one should be runnable tomorrow by a person who has read only
the dossier.
